Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen keeps cooking.

Hotels & Multifamily

Hot Water for Hotels, Apartments, and Large Facilities

In a hotel, apartment building, or large facility, hot water demand runs around the clock, with sharp morning peaks when dozens of showers run at once, and the scale of the load calls for specialized experience and a structured plan. Harrisburg Water Heater handles high-demand commercial systems, deploying experienced technicians on banks of commercial tanks, racked tankless systems, boiler-fed storage tanks, and recirculation loops built for continuous operation.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched fast for emergency repairs anywhere in Harrisburg and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be done after hours to limit downtime.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

A maintenance program from Harrisburg Water Heater gets built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the tap. Mineral-heavy water shortens the life of commercial equipment fast, so busy properties usually benefit from more frequent visits.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections.
