Keeping Your Lemoyne Water Heater Running Longer

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year in hard-water areas.

Take a look at the age of your water heater. The serial number on the label usually encodes the manufacture date, and its age says a lot about what comes next. Most tank models reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units around 20. If your heater is past that range and is also showing discolored water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement once the tank fails.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a surprise cleanup.

Hot Water Headaches Lemoyne Homeowners Face

In Lemoyne, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric units lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
